There is no Grand Design,  
It's just a struggle, called a "Life".  
And it's contestants, called people  
Just go away, passing by.

Why should I be clear when my mind is cloudy?  
Why should I rely on, and trust my self to somebody?  
Only to be then thrown away?  
Only to grasp on the empty air,  
When it comes, that I was left behind.
